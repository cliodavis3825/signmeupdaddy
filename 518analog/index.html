About the club

We started 518 Analog because the best evenings are the ones where everybody looks up. Come once, come every week, bring a friend or come alone and meet someone. Beginners are the point, not the exception: we'll teach you the game.

  • Where: around the Capital Region (each event lists its spot)
  • Who: grown-ups of every age and experience level
  • Cost: joining is free; some events pass the hat for supplies
  • Signing up: takes about 30 seconds, and you can cancel any time

Upcoming events

Pick an event, tap Sign up, and you'll get a confirmation email. Seats are limited, so a spot you can't use is a gift to someone on the waitlist: please cancel from your email if plans change.

What we do

Book Club

One book, one evening, lots of opinions. Fiction, memoir, the occasional page-turner nobody will admit to loving.

Mahjong

Learn-as-you-play tables for newcomers and open tables for old hands. Tiles and snacks provided.

Trivia

Teams of friendly strangers, questions from every decade. Bragging rights are the only prize.

Craft Nights

Knit, stitch, paint or just make a mess. Bring your own project or try ours.

Join us

  1. Add yourself to the email list. That's how you join. We'll email you when new events go up.
  2. Pick an event from the calendar above and sign up with your name and email.
  3. Show up and say hi. We'll handle the rest.

Questions before you come? Ask on the day or use the report form below; a real person reads everything.

Report an issue

Something broken on the site, a signup that didn't go through, or a problem at an event? Tell us and we'll sort it out.

Report a website problem

Website problems go to our public issue tracker (free GitHub account needed). Please don't include personal details there.
