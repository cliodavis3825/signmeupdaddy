Free event signups for clubs

Sign up. Show up.

SignMeUpDaddy is a simple home for club events: book club, mahjong night, trivia, craft circles. Members pick an event and grab a seat in under a minute. Organizers get a roster, a waitlist and reminders, with nothing to install.

How it works

  1. Find your club. Search by name and open its calendar.
  2. Pick an event and sign up. Just a name and email. No account, no password.
  3. Get your confirmation. You'll get an email with a calendar invite and a link to cancel if plans change. A seat you can't use goes straight to the next person on the waitlist.
  • Seat limits and automatic waitlists
  • Reminder emails the day before
  • Calendar feed for Apple, Google and Outlook
  • Organizer roster, check-in and table seating
